# pawlins-scrolly-story-final
  I created this presentation to be a celebration of the human and animal bond. Originally, it was just supposed to be based upon the data I had meticulously gathered at work. Then I would take that data and use that to specifically further my story, which was just going to be aboiut the kinds of breeds that were my favorite and why that was the case. However, once I learned we needed to draw upon some of our readings for this presentation, I began to think about what I could say about this in regards to the humanities. Once I remembered our readings about AI and their lack of human understanding, it allowed me to truly take this presentation into a whole other level. 
  This meant that I was able to pour so much emotion and heart into the last 4 slides, making everything that happened before seem relatively tame in comparison. I do not regret any of my choices about the project regardless. I needed to build off of my data but also lead into the digital humanities tie-in, and I think I handled all of in it in genuinely the best way I could. I am really glad I was able to write that last section because it helped me have my ultimate takeaway from the entire class. This being the importance of the intersectionality of the humanities and the digital world that result in digital humanities. 
  More specifically, just how important that human element is, and how important it is to never let that go. I am really glad I chose to write about the AI readings, specifically because our ability to form attachments is something they do not have and will never understand. It is what seperates us from them and reinforces quite heavily the beauty of humanity. With this current wave of AI evolving at an unprecendented rate, it is hard not to see Digital Humanities as a rejection of AI as a whole. 
  Let's say things get bad, like REAL bad. Let's say AI take over the planet, let's say everything becomes digitized. The main things that will remain that truly reflect the lives of humans will come from the humanities. I know that was disturbing, so I won't talk about that anymore, but I wanted to paint a picture of just how important the humanities are when it comes to the preservation of every culture in the entire world. Represented or unrepresented. 
  Overall, I loved putting together this presentation. I see it as a celebration of what I am currently doing for a living, in conjunction with both my fears of AI and my hope in the power of human bonds and connections. With how bleak things seem to be in the world so often I wanted to put together something that could celebrate the innocent and pure. Because at the at the end of the day, that's what dogs are, they have no evil intentions in their heart. Right now, I feel we all could use even more of that than ever before.
